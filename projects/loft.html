Bed loft

2024–2026

I worked on this project with my dad from 2024 to 2026 and we finally finished it before this school year started. We didn't expect it to take as long as it did, but ran into many complications including warped wood that we had to flatten with a router sled (not pictured) and having to redesign the loft with the wall ledgers in order to bear enough weight on the window side where there was no wall joist to support the floating beam. At the end of the day, it is the largest (and most expensive) engineering and architectural project I have finished, and I learned many things from cutting steel with an oxy-acetylene torch to running architectural calculations to calculate whether our design has an acceptable safety factor (it's rock solid, and can bear an ~1000-pound point load in the center).
