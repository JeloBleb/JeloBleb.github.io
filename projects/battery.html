Custom 8S battery

I decided to build my own battery packs because I was getting into FPV, and it saves a lot of money. I ended up learning some spot welding (running extreme current through metal strips to weld them) and battery basics (lead balancing to ensure all cells stay at the same voltage, battery discharge curves, etc.). I made a few mistakes that forced me to rebuild and shorted the cells more times than I could count (it happens surprisingly easily, it can be done with a soldering iron, pliers, wiring the metal strips incorrectly, and more). At the end of the day, I probably saved a hundred bucks and built some extra high voltage packs, which I talk about in the drone project tab.
